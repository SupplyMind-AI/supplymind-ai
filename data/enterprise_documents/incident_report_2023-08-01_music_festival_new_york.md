# Incident Report — Music Festival Road Closures, DC-NYC (INC-2023-052)

## Summary

From 1–3 August 2023, a large multi-day music festival in New York City closed roads around the venue and drew heavily on the local casual labor pool. Known weeks in advance, it was handled proactively as a planned public event (severity 0.48, Moderate). Unlike the parade (INC-2023-038), this event affected both routing and staff availability over three days. Handled under `disruption_response_playbook.md`.

## Timeline

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Jul 18 | Festival dates confirmed; added to DC-NYC calendar; staffing plan reviewed |
| Jul 30 | Venue-area closures mapped; three-day dispatch plan finalized |
| Aug 01–03 | Pre-positioned volume; routes avoid venue corridors; staffing pre-booked |
| Aug 04 | Closures clear; normal operations resume |

## Affected scope

- DC-NYC outbound routes near the venue over three days.
- Reduced casual labor availability across the festival weekend.
- Roughly 500 shipments managed under the proactive plan; no accounts at risk.

## Root cause

A planned, publicized multi-day event with both road closures and local labor competition. Predictable, so the response combined routing and staffing preparation.

## Mitigation

1. **Pre-positioning and rerouting** — staged and dispatched venue-adjacent volume early; routed around closure corridors for the weekend.
2. **Staff pre-booking** — secured warehouse staffing ahead of the weekend rather than relying on casual labor competing with the festival.
3. **Cut-off communication** — adjusted weekend Standard Class cut-offs with advance customer notice.
4. **Calendar control** — treated the multi-day window as a scheduled operational condition.

## Impact

- DC-NYC on-time held at 96.9% across the weekend; no credits, no breaches.
- Confirmed that staffing, not just routing, must be planned for multi-day city events.
